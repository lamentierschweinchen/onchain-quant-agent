### Analysis

THE CHAIN STOPPED. The last block on shard 0 is timestamped 2026-09-19 07:37 UTC, shard 1 07:37 UTC, shard 2 07:39 UTC and the metachain 07:41 UTC. The snapshot was taken 55 hours later and no shard had advanced. The epoch counter reads 2241 (+5 on the week) and the chain processed 1,008,370 transactions before it stopped, about 189,533 a day over the 5.3 days it ran.

WHAT HAPPENED, FROM THE CHAIN. Wallet erd1kwvkch79...rvvjd2 received 16.8 EGLD on Sep 16 (from MEXC and Bybit withdrawal wallets and one other address), then on Sep 19 deployed contract erd1qqqq...ke2kq9 at 06:34 UTC, created an NFT, and began a loop: deposit EGLD into the contract, call compound, move the resulting token to itself, repeat. The deposits doubled every round, 30 deposit rounds in all. At the end the wallet held 41,307,412 EGLD and the contract 25,559,887. Doubling a 0.05 EGLD stake thirty-odd times gives numbers of that size, which fits MultiversX's description of an atomicity flaw in the VM: the balance produced by a failed or partial step was kept. The model reports the flows and leaves the mechanism to MultiversX's technical report.

From 07:24 to 07:27 UTC the wallet sent 9,812,376 EGLD to 12 fresh addresses, each of which forwarded its whole receipt within minutes: Binance.com 2,288,296, KuCoin 1,821,376, MEXC 750,203, the unlabelled wallet erd1a6lte0 1,634,870, and Unknown Whale I 775,154. Three fresh wallets still hold 2,542,478. Block production stopped about ten minutes after the last forward. The total supply field on /economics still reads 30,816,932, so the protocol's own accounting has not absorbed the minted balances.

WHAT MULTIVERSX AND THE EXCHANGES SAID. MultiversX: an actor attempted to exploit a VM-level atomicity issue that caused invalid state changes; network progression is paused; a fix is being validated on a shadow fork and will be deployed in coordination with validators, exchanges and infrastructure partners; a targeted recovery that preserves finalized history and legitimate user state is being evaluated; attacker accounts are identified and frozen with exchanges. Upbit suspended EGLD deposits and withdrawals on Sep 19 and designated EGLD a trading-caution market on Sep 21 (review through Oct 19-23); Bithumb suspended transfers; Kraken set EGLD to cancel-only. Those points come from press reports of MultiversX's statement and the exchanges' notices, not from the chain.

PRICE. EGLD closed the snapshot at $4.24 (+2.42% on the week). The hourly series shows the path: $4.06 at the halt, a low of $3.56 on Sep 20 15:00 UTC, then a full recovery to $4.24 while BTC rose +5.8% from the halt. The market is pricing a successful recovery.
