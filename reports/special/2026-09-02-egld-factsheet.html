2 A large bet against the move that has not yet been forced to close Leveraged positions equal 34% of the token's entire market value, and 27 of 32 venues show short sellers paying to hold. If they are forced to buy back, that accelerates the move sharply. If buying stops, the same leverage works in reverse. high, unresolved
3 The Supernova network upgrade on 10 September Block times drop from 6 seconds to 0.6 seconds. Eight days away. It is the only dated catalyst — but the price was already up 97% before this session, so it accelerated a move that started three weeks earlier rather than causing it. accelerant

Bottom line

Real, broad buying of the coin is doing the work, and it is the widest participation in twenty-three weeks of tracking. Sitting on top of it is a leveraged bet against the move worth a third of the token's market value that has not yet been forced to unwind. That makes the next move larger in whichever direction it goes. The dated risk is the upgrade itself on 10 September: the rally is three weeks older than the catalyst, and everyone can see the date.

Terms

Open interest
Total value of leveraged bets currently open. High relative to market cap means a small price move forces large buying or selling.
Funding rate
Periodic fee between the two sides of a leveraged trade. Negative means those betting on a fall are paying those betting on a rise.
OTC desk
A wallet that fills large orders privately rather than on an exchange order book. Its stock of coins is supply waiting to be sold.
Withdrawal breadth
How many separate wallets took coins off exchanges. Many small withdrawals suggests broad demand; few large ones suggests one buyer.
Staking
Locking coins to help secure the network in exchange for yield. Locked coins cannot be sold quickly.
Supernova
MultiversX protocol upgrade activating 10 September, cutting block time from 6 seconds to 0.6 seconds.
